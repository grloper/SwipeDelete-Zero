# SwipeDelete Zero privacy policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy describes the **Google Play edition** of SwipeDelete Zero (`com.swipedelete.zero`).

## Data used on your device

With your permission, the app reads your selected or permitted photos, videos and audio so you can review them, find possible duplicates and clutter, and choose what to keep or delete. It stores review decisions, exclusions, analysis results and cleanup history in local app storage. Android asks you to confirm supported deletion or trash operations. The Play edition does not create an account, show ads, run analytics, or collect or transmit your files, identifiers or usage information to the developer. It does not request internet access.

You can deny media access, choose limited access where Android offers it, or revoke access in Android Settings. You can restore a staged item before confirming deletion. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local database; files in your Android library are unaffected by uninstalling. Files moved to Android Trash are managed by Android's retention rules. Permanently deleted files cannot be restored by this app.

## Other editions

An independently distributed **cloud edition** offers optional Google Drive and Google Photos uploads after you connect your own Google account. It has an internet permission and is not the edition described above or planned for Google Play. See [cloud setup](DRIVE_BACKUP_SETUP.md) for those features.

## Contact

For questions or privacy requests, open an issue at [SwipeDelete Zero on GitHub](https://github.com/grloper/SwipeDelete-Zero/issues). Because the Play edition does not receive your data, there is no server-side profile for us to erase.
